Listing-short forward test — 4 arms, 3 venues

Five pre-registered out-of-sample replications put this effect on large-audience venues and not on small ones, at +3.46% per trade across 111 clean events — sitting on its significance bar, not past it. Each arm listens to one venue and trades the same Gate USDT perpetual, so the venue supplies only the listing timestamp. Every arm was declared in advance and none may be dropped. The cap is applied here and not when collecting trades. Amendment 1 of the pre-registration established that a gate may size down and must never skip an event, because skipping removes sample points and biases the test. So every arm above takes every eligible listing, and this panel shows what a single account running at most 1 position at a time would have earned from the same record. On the clean historical samples a cap of 1 returned +64.4% CAGR at an 11.2% drawdown against +59.9% at 19.0% for a cap of 2 — simultaneous shorts on new listings are correlated, so a second open position is leverage, not diversification.
